# TIG Torch Parts Catalog Reference

## Purpose

This internal record supports the public TIG torch parts category, identification guide and RFQ
builder. It separates torch-series and component references visibly documented in the company
catalog from compatibility or technical claims that still require evidence from the buyer.

## Primary Source

- File: `public/downloads/renqiu-ailesen-welding-catalog.pdf`
- Company shown: Renqiu Ailesen Welding Technology Co., Ltd.
- PDF pages reviewed: 25-36
- Printed catalog pages shown in the spreads: 43-66
- Visual review date: 2026-08-12

## Documented TIG References

| Catalog reference group | Components visibly identified in the catalog                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| WP-9 / WP-20            | Torch bodies, back caps, collets, collet bodies, gas lenses, ceramic nozzles or cups, handles, switches and cable assemblies                                   |
| WP-17 / WP-18 / WP-26   | Torch bodies, back caps, collets, collet bodies, gas lenses, ceramic nozzles or cups, handles, switches, cable or hose assemblies and termination arrangements |
| WP-27                   | Torch body, collets, gas lenses, ceramic nozzles or cups, cable or hose assembly and multiple termination arrangements                                         |

The catalog also shows component-level tables with part references, bores, cup sizes, dimensions,
threads, cable lengths and torch technical data. These values are not generalized in the category
RFQ builder. A buyer must first identify the exact torch series and assembly before a documented
value can be checked against the requested product.

## Publication Boundaries

- The group names describe the company's documented catalog range and sourcing vocabulary.
- Grouped catalog tables do not prove that every component is interchangeable across all listed
  torch configurations.
- Do not infer cooling arrangement, electrical rating, tungsten range, thread, cable length or
  machine connection from the series name alone.
- Do not restore the retired TIG Torch Switch product page until a dedicated switch image and source
  evidence support that specific product.
- Use `catalog reference group` publicly and require the exact label, drawing, approved sample or
  complete component stack for final compatibility review.

## Buyer Evidence Required

1. Complete TIG torch photo and model label before disassembly.
2. Ceramic cup, collet body or gas lens, collet, tungsten and back cap in removal order.
3. Cup number, visible part reference and documented tungsten diameter where available.
4. Thread, connection, cable or hose and machine-side termination views.
5. Drawing, approved sample or repeat-order reference when available.
6. Quantity by part, standard or OEM packing requirement and destination country.
