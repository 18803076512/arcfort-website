# MIG Torch Liner Selection Guide - Source Record

Reviewed: 2026-08-09

## Scope

This record supports the public buyer guide at `/guides/mig-torch-liner-selection-guide`. It is an
editorial traceability note, not a compatibility table or an ArcFort Weld product specification.

## Public Claims Allowed

- A MIG/MAG torch liner guides welding wire through the gun cable toward the contact tip.
- Liner range should be checked against welding wire diameter.
- Wire type, cable length, connector or termination and torch reference are relevant ordering fields.
- Metallic and polymer liner constructions serve different wire-delivery requirements.
- Compatibility should be confirmed by model, sample, drawing or approved documentation.

## Claims Deliberately Excluded

- Universal liner material rules for every wire or torch.
- Exact liner bore, trim length, installation clearance or maintenance interval.
- Confirmed compatibility with any third-party brand or model.
- Unverified OEM numbers, product ratings, certifications, prices or stock status.
