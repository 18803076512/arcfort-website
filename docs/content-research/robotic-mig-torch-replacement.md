# Robotic MIG/MAG Torch Replacement Guide - Source Record

Reviewed: 2026-08-13

## Scope

This record supports the public product page at
`/products/welding-accessories/robot-welding-torch` and the buyer guide at
`/guides/robotic-mig-welding-torch-replacement-guide`. It documents industry terminology and the
evidence buyers should provide. It is not a compatibility table or an ArcFort Weld product
specification.

## Public Claims Allowed

- A robotic MIG/MAG torch replacement inquiry should define the requested replacement boundary.
- Interface and torch-neck geometry, cooling, cable arrangement, connectors and consumables are
  relevant evidence for replacement review.
- A product-family photo does not prove a complete robot flange, mount, cable package or cooling
  circuit.
- Compatibility should be reviewed against the installed torch reference, approved drawing,
  current-part evidence or physical sample.
- Trial approval and retained repeat-order references help buyers control replacement purchasing.

## Claims Deliberately Excluded

- Compatibility with any named robot, welding equipment or torch brand.
- Exact TCP, neck angle, dimensions, connectors, cooling type, rating or duty cycle.
- Inclusion of a robot flange, collision mount, cable package, cooling circuit or control cable.
- Unverified OEM numbers, certifications, prices, stock status or performance claims.
