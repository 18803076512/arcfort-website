# MIG Gas Nozzle and Diffuser Selection Guide - Source Record

Reviewed: 2026-08-09

## Scope

This record supports the public buyer guide at
`/guides/mig-gas-nozzle-diffuser-selection-guide`. It is an editorial traceability note, not a
compatibility table or an ArcFort Weld product specification.

## Public Claims Allowed

- A MIG/MAG gas nozzle directs shielding gas around the wire and weld area.
- Nozzle profile, opening, attachment and position are relevant ordering fields.
- Diffuser, tip-holder and tip-adaptor terminology can vary by consumable system.
- Nozzles, diffusers, insulators and contact tips should be checked as a connected front-end system.
- Compatibility should be confirmed by model, manufacturer document, drawing, sample or approved
  reference.

## Claims Deliberately Excluded

- Universal nozzle geometry, contact-tip position or replacement interval.
- Guaranteed MB15 or third-party brand compatibility.
- Exact dimensions, threads, material grades or product ratings not supplied by confirmed records.
- Unverified OEM numbers, certifications, prices, stock status or performance claims.
