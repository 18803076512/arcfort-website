# Plasma Consumables Catalog Reference

## Purpose

This internal record supports the public plasma cutting consumables category and buyer guide. It
separates product-family references visibly documented in the company catalog from compatibility or
technical claims that still require buyer evidence.

## Primary Source

- File: `public/downloads/renqiu-ailesen-welding-catalog.pdf`
- Company shown: Renqiu Ailesen Welding Technology Co., Ltd.
- PDF pages reviewed: 22-24

- Visual review date: 2026-08-12

## Publication Boundaries

- The family names describe the company's documented catalog range.
- They do not prove that every part within a named family is interchangeable.
- Do not convert a catalog family into an exact compatibility statement without the buyer's torch
  label, approved part reference, drawing, sample or complete matched stack.
- Do not infer nozzle opening, current, dimensions, material grade or technical rating from a photo.
- Use the public wording `catalog reference family` and state that final compatibility requires
  review.

## Buyer Evidence Required

1. Plasma torch label and complete torch view.
2. Assembled front-end photograph before disassembly.
3. Electrode, nozzle, ring, cap and shield in removal order.
4. Both ends, connections, profiles and visible markings for each requested part.
5. Existing part number, approved drawing or reliable measured sample where available.
6. Quantity by component, packing requirement and destination country.
