# MIG/MAG Torch Parts Catalog Reference - Source Record

Reviewed: 2026-08-13

## Scope

This record supports the company-catalog reference matrix and interactive RFQ builder on
`/products/mig-mag-torch-parts`. It documents product-family coverage in the Renqiu Ailesen
welding catalog. It is not a universal compatibility table and does not independently confirm a
specific buyer torch, OEM number, dimension, rating or material.

## Company Catalog Evidence

- PDF pages 4-6 (printed catalog pages 1-6) show ORK 200A, 350A and 500A complete torch breakdowns
  with front-end wear parts, liner and connection references.
- PDF pages 7-8 (printed catalog pages 7-10) show two 15AK arrangements with nozzle, contact tip,
  tip holder, insulator or holder spring, swan neck, liner and torch-body references.
- PDF pages 9-10 (printed catalog pages 11-14) show 24KD and 25AK torch breakdowns with nozzles,
  contact tips, tip holders, gas diffusers, swan necks, liners and rear connections.
- PDF pages 11-12 (printed catalog pages 15-18) show 36KD and 40KD torch breakdowns with their own
  front-end and liner references.
- PDF pages 13-14 (printed catalog pages 19-22) show water-cooled 501D and 602 arrangements with
  front-end parts, cable and water connections.
- Later MIG/MAG catalog pages also show additional systems, connectors and automatic-torch parts.
  They are deliberately excluded from the first RFQ selector to keep the buyer path concise.

## Public Claims Allowed

- The company catalog documents the five reference groups used on the category page.
- The documented assemblies include product families such as gas nozzles, contact tips, tip
  holders, gas diffusers or insulators, swan necks, liners and selected connection parts.
- A buyer should identify the exact series and submit the complete front-end stack, model label,
  connection photos, drawing or sample before compatibility is confirmed.
- Catalog series can be used to organize an RFQ without treating the series name as proof of fit.

## Claims Deliberately Excluded

- Universal compatibility with a third-party brand, torch or machine.
- Interchangeability between 15AK, 24KD, 25AK, 36KD, 40KD, 501D, 602 or ORK assemblies.
- Unverified OEM numbers, exact dimensions, ratings, material grades or performance claims.
- Certifications, prices, stock status, production capacity or customer use cases.

## RFQ Confirmation Boundary

The buyer should submit the torch label, a complete torch photo, front-end parts in removal order,
visible markings, documented wire diameter, required quantity and rear connection views. For
water-cooled assemblies, include the complete water and cable connection layout. ArcFort Weld can
then review the requested combination before issuing a quotation.
