## Physical Data Is Not Commercial Policy

Keep the package method, explicit count and explicit unit together. A bag, inner box and master
carton are different scopes: never infer hierarchy, multiply counts or copy a nearby SKU's package.
Unknown counts remain unknown, not zero or a suggested factory value. The current 43 imported
records have no quantity/unit evidence and remain `NEEDS_FACTORY_CONFIRMATION`.

MOQ, trial-order policy, OEM MOQ and lead-time notes are commercial statements. Retain their
original authority and owner-approval requirements separately from physical packaging sources.
A packaging photo, carton label, catalog or source-intake receipt does not confirm commercial terms,
usage rights, exact-product fit, compatibility, current stock or readiness to publish.

## Evidence Handoff

Collect the exact SKU, package description, quantity/unit when genuinely supplied, source owner,
document reference/version/location/date and whether the source supports, contradicts or merely
references the candidate. Retain the original packaging row when explicitly correcting it, rather
than silently substituting a new source. Each source remains immutable and tied to the exact copy.

A declared qualifying Level A basis prepares human review; an actual authorized reviewer still
must inspect evidence, resolve contradictions and approve the frozen proposal. Company catalogs,
OEM/manufacturer packaging and standards retain their reference scope. Do not present synthetic
test descriptions, quantities, custodians or declarations as actual ArcFort evidence.

D1 currently provides private source intake only. There is no packaging editor, approval decision,
current-value projection or public consumer yet. Continue those required steps and real service
acceptance before describing packaging management as usable or the real 15AK pilot as completed.
